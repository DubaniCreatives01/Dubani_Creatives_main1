The studio

Led by Silulamele Dubani, from Cape Town.

Dubani Creatives is a graphic design studio. Silulamele Dubani founded it in 2020 and still leads the work. We speak as a studio because projects are delivered that way: one clear lead, and the collaborators a job needs.

The organisations we design for include businesses, NGOs, community trusts, property and housing groups, churches and events. Much of the work is identity, print and websites that have to be used by staff, not only admired in a presentation.

A project we can talk about

FCI Community Housing Services came to the studio as DCI. We renamed and rebranded the organisation as FCI, set up the domain and website at fcichs.co.za, produced the brand pack, company profile, email signatures and application forms, cleaned up the site after it was hacked, moved the hosting, and stayed on as the monthly team for the website and social media.
