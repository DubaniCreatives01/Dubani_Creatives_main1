How a project runs

A short path from brief to files.

1. Listen

We start with a free consultation and a written brief: who the work is for, what must change, and what already exists.

2. Make

We design, revise with you, and prepare the print, social or website files the project needs.

3. Stay

Some projects end with a handover. Others continue as a monthly retainer for the website, social media, or both.
