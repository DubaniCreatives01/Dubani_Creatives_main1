My Story

Giving organisations design that is clear, consistent, and properly made.

I started Dubani Creatives in 2020 to give businesses and community organisations design that communicates with clarity. You deal directly with the person designing your work, from the first brief to the final file.

Over the past six years, I have worked with diverse clients ranging from non-profit foundations and property developers to corporate legal consultancies. Notable partnerships include FCI Community Housing Services, whose full brand overhaul, website, and ongoing monthly communications I look after, as well as the Khayelitsha Community Trust (KCT).
