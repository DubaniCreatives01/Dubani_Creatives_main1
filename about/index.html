My story

I started Dubani Creatives in 2020 to give businesses and community organisations design that is clear, consistent and properly made. You deal directly with the person designing your work, from the first brief to the final file. Clients include FCI Community Housing Services, whose rebrand, website and monthly content I look after, and Khayelitsha Community Trust.
