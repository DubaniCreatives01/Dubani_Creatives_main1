Services

Design that staff can actually use.

Five kinds of work, plus monthly retainers when a brand needs someone to keep showing up. Every engagement starts with a conversation, not a template.

01

Brand identity and logos

A name, a mark, colours, type and a small set of rules. For FCI this was a full rebrand from DCI, including the new name. For Regimed Health it was brand architecture across a group of sub-brands. For CashWave and Makinzi Building Solutions it started with the logo and the stationery around it.

02

Print and marketing collateral

Annual reports, company profiles, signage, banners, hoardings, stationery and calendars. Khayelitsha Community Trust’s annual reports from 2022 to 2025, the KCT Mall brand refresh, signage master plan, hoardings and stationery, and the Social Impact report sit in this work. CashWave’s gazebo, flags, banners, trailer banner and calendars do too.

03

Social media content and management

Campaign graphics, event posters and menus, and ongoing management. Festa M’s EP cover, EPK and concert posters, Mellows Lounge’s menus and event posters, and Gugulethu Sports Council’s banners and posters are examples. FCI’s social media is managed monthly.

04

Custom websites

Design, build, hosting and maintenance. We have built and cared for sites including fcichs.co.za, Regimed Health and Langa Life Bible Church. When FCI’s site was hacked, the work included security cleanup and a hosting migration, then the forms people use to apply.

05

Creative direction

Deciding what a brand should look like across print, events and digital before the individual files are made, and keeping later work inside that decision.

06

Monthly retainers

A standing arrangement for website care, social media, or both. FCI Community Housing Services is on a monthly retainer for website and social media management. Retainers are scoped to the organisation. They are not a fixed public price.
