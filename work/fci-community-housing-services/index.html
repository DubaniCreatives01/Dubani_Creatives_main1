FCI Community Housing Services

A rebrand from DCI to FCI, carried through a comprehensive brand pack, company profile and custom website, and maintained every month.

ClientFCI Community Housing
Timeline2024 – Present (Retainer)
ServicesBrand, Web, Profile, Social

The Brief

Transitioning with clarity

FCI needed to transition cleanly from their previous institutional identity (DCI) to FCI Community Housing Services, requiring an authoritative and approachable visual identity that would resonate with residents, municipal partners, and housing stakeholders.

The Approach

Unified across every touchpoint

I designed the new FCI brand identity and comprehensive asset pack, produced the corporate company profile, and engineered the new corporate website from the ground up to ensure the name change felt seamless, professional, and consistent across digital and physical materials.

The Result & Retainer

Ongoing monthly impact

The organisation launched with full brand consistency. Today, on an active monthly retainer, I manage ongoing website updates, tenant notices, and social media communication assets to keep their channels continuously sharp.
