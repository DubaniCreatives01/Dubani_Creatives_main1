Work · Case study

FCI Community Housing Services

A rebrand from DCI to FCI, a new website, and the documents and care the organisation needed afterwards.

The situation

FCI Community Housing Services needed to leave the DCI name behind. The organisation required a new name, a new domain, and a public identity that could carry a website, a company profile, email signatures and application forms.

The website was also hacked. It needed a security cleanup, a move to new hosting, and working forms before it could be handed back as a site staff could rely on.

What we did

  • Rebranded the organisation from DCI to FCI, including the new name
  • Set up the new domain and website at fcichs.co.za
  • Produced the brand pack and company profile
  • Designed email signatures and application forms
  • Cleaned up the website after the hack and migrated the hosting
  • Continued with website and social media management on a monthly retainer

Where it stands

The FCI name is in use and the website is live at fcichs.co.za. The brand pack, company profile, email signatures and application forms were part of the rebrand. Website and social media management continues on a monthly retainer.

Fezile Calana, CEO of FCI Community Housing Services, said he was thoroughly impressed.
